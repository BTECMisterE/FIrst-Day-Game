🦠 First Day BreakOut — Run-of-Show

A get-to-know-you mingle game that turns into a contact-tracing mystery. One senior + one object are dusted with UV powder; the app secretly logs who met whom. Total time ≈ 35–45 min.

Prep Before students arrive ~10 min

  1. Prep your powder delivery: dust the sign-in pen (hand it to only your chosen senior) or keep a pinch of powder in your palm for a “welcome back!” handshake — so you can pick Patient Zero in the moment, once you see who’s here.
  2. Powder the object: dust one high-traffic item — sign-in tablet, sanitizer pump, candy bowl, door handle.
  3. Open the app → I’m the Instructor — Host. Put the URL + 4-letter room code on the projector (or the 📲 Join QR poster).
  4. In the app, mark the object you dusted as SOURCE ☣️ — it’s just your answer key. The object has no code and isn’t tracked; students find it by observation.

1 The mingle (disguised ice breaker) 15–20 min

  • Pick Patient Zero now that you can see who came. Students are anonymous (codenames only) — ask your senior for the codename on their phone and tap set, or ⭐ the seniors and press 🎲 Random senior. Then powder that person (dusted pen / handshake). Students never see this.
  • Press Start the mingle.
  • Students get missions, chat, swap 4-letter codes, and record a new fact about each person. Goal: meet everyone.
  • Quietly make sure everyone touches the dusted object (“sign in here,” “grab a candy”) so the powder spreads — nothing to log, it’s physical only.
  • Watch coverage % + the “still need to mingle” nudge list. End when you’re happy.
“Meet as many people as you can — and learn one new thing about each!”

2 The reveal (the surprise) 3–5 min

  • Press Reveal the outbreak.
  • Kill the lights. Turn on the blacklight. Let them find the glow on their hands.
  • Expect most of the room to glow — that’s the lesson: one person + one object → the whole class.
“One person and one object this morning did all of this. Now — can you prove where it started, like a real disease detective?”

3 The investigation 8–12 min

  • Press Open the investigation. Each phone shows the line list (symptom-onset order) + contact log.
  • In teams, students reason:
    • Patient Zero = earliest onset (top of the list) + dossier to unmask the codename.
    • The object = observation — “what did all the glowing hands touch?”
  • Stuck / everyone knows each other? Use the 🔍 Drip a clue button — it pushes the facts classmates recorded about Patient Zero to every screen, one at a time, until the room shouts out who it is.
  • Teams Submit our answer (a person + an object).

4 The finale 2 min

  • Press 🎯 Reveal the answer — every phone shows the truth and whether each team was right.
  • Confirm it was your returning senior 🔴 + the object ☣️.
“This is contact tracing — the exact logic public-health teams use to stop real outbreaks. Welcome to the field.”

🛟 If something goes sideways

No room codeCodes are 4 letters, case-insensitive. Don’t press “End & reset” — it closes the room.
Can’t find a codeThey must ask — that’s the ice breaker. Codes are on each student’s own screen.
Can’t spot the objectNudge: “everyone’s glowing — what’s the one thing you all touched coming in?” It’s observation, not an app lookup.
No clues to dripFacts weren’t logged about the senior. Fall back to the line list + dossier.
Want fewer infectedEnd the mingle early — less of the room glows (spot-the-sick version).
